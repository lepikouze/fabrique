{% elif tab == 'git' %}{% set git, row = data.git %}

Git providers

Where the repos live. Several providers of the same type are possible (two GitLab instances, or the same GitLab with two bot accounts): each has its name, and each repo is attached to one of them. Two providers may share an address, not a token. The token belongs to a bot account dedicated to Fabrique: it clones, pushes branches, opens and merges PRs. It is encrypted, never shown again, and handed to the runner for each job. Protect the main branch of your repos: the bot must be able to merge a PR, not push directly. Without a token here, the runner uses its environment variables (GIT_TOKEN, GITLAB_TOKEN, GITHUB_TOKEN).

Budgets and gates

Enforced by the runner and by the dashboard, never by a prompt. A repo can have stricter values.

Archives only merged jobs and studies already read. Failures and refused PRs stay visible. An archived job can be found and restored from the Archives page. The daily budget is enforced by the dashboard: beyond it, new work waits for the next day; merges already decided go through. Study reports Unticked, a box removes the button from the job and the inbox, and the download is refused. The report stays readable in Fabrique. A PR that touches one of them never merges on its own. Fabrique's recipe (.fabrique/**) and the CI are always among them. {{ trace(row) }}

Job language

Everyone chooses their interface language in the menu at the bottom left. This page sets the job language: the language of texts written for other readers.

In this language: what agents and reviewers write for people (understanding, assumptions, opinion, questions, study report, review points), what Fabrique writes on the Git host (PR, review guide, comments, initialization commits) and notifications. A repo can choose its own in its settings. Each reader keeps the interface in their own language, and can have a job's texts translated.
